A principle of responsible adoption

Do not take an important concept or anything as an absolute truth in the mortal world simply because it came from thousands of years ago, from sages, scholars, social reformers or forefathers, or because it has been seen and believed since birth.

Instead, take the concept into the soul, heart and mind, test it and logically analyse it with respect to selfless moral human values. If the soul, heart and mind agree with the concept or ideology, follow or adopt it in the true spirit, and then improve it step-by-step without harming anyone.
